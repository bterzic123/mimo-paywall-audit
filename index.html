What this is

We went through Mimo as a new learner, up to the paywall and what happens when you close it.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and this year we published what 78,000+ of their paywalls tell us in State of Paywalls 2026. The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes come before cosmetic ones, because that is what wins most often in the report. Each idea links straight to its screen in the prototype.

01

Name the yearly plan and show its price

Your cards read “Free Trial · €0.00” and “Welcome Offer · 4,99 €”. Neither one says yearly, and the 74,99 € a year only shows up in the small print under the button. Call the first card Yearly, put 74,99 € and 6,24 € a month on it, and keep the 7 free days as its badge.

A/B tests
02

Put your 4.8 stars on the paywall

Mimo has a 4.8 from 110K ratings in the US App Store, and your listing quotes The New York Times. The paywall shows none of it. The only proof in the flow is a testimonial on the screen before the paywall.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026Only 27% of top paywalls show social proof. On the paywall, it lifted conversion in winning tests. See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning
A/B tests
03

Use their onboarding answers in the headline

Onboarding asks why they’re learning, which path they want (Front-End, Full-Stack…) and how long they’ll practice each day. Then the paywall says “The first week of Pro are on us” to everyone, with a small slip in the grammar. Try naming their path and their daily minutes instead: “Your Front-End path is ready · 10 min a day”.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026Only 2% of top paywalls use what the app knows about the user. See the screen CR +8–17%Observed in winning
A/B tests
04

Show the saving on your offer, not a code

Very few apps have a second offer, and you do. But it shows a code, WELCOME25OFF, which reads like 25% off and looks like something you have to type. The price it gives is 59,99 € a year against 74,99 €, which is 20% less. Say that in plain words: “20% off Pro, 59,99 € a year instead of 74,99 €”.
